# BottleBazaar Customer User Guide

Welcome to **BottleBazaar**, India's premier discovery and discount marketplace for spirits, wine, and craft beer.

## 1. Age Declaration & Onboarding
- When opening the app for the first time, you must select your state and confirm your birth year.
- In Maharashtra and Delhi, the legal drinking age is **25**. In Karnataka and Goa, it is **21**.
- If you are below the legal drinking age in your state, access is automatically restricted.

## 2. Browsing Stores & Offers
- **Discover Tab**: View curated seasonal campaigns, browse by categories (Whiskey, Beer, Wine, Gin, Vodka, Rum), and see nearby verified shops.
- **Stores Tab**: Search retail shops by area or city. View exact addresses, excise licenses, open hours, and active deals.
- **Deals Tab**: Filter discounts by percentage off, flat rupee discount, or BOGO offers.

## 3. Claiming & Redeeming Discounts
1. Tap **"Claim In-Store Coupon"** on any active deal.
2. Sign in with your mobile phone number using SMS OTP (or dev bypass `123456`).
3. Your coupon is issued with a 6-digit alphanumeric code (e.g. `BB-948123`) and a QR code in your **Wallet Tab**.
4. Visit the physical liquor retail outlet.
5. **Before paying**, show your QR code or 6-digit voucher to the cashier.
6. The cashier enters the code into their BottleBazaar terminal, verifying the discount against your bill amount.

## 4. Responsible Drinking Support
If you or anyone you know requires confidential alcohol support, use the in-app link to call the National Alcohol Helpline: **1800-11-0031**.
